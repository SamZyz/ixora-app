Alerts

For when the screen is out of sight.

When you start a Study, Exam, Blind or Talk timer, this page adds a one-minute event at the end time to your Google Calendar with a pop-up reminder. Your phone or iPad then notifies you through the Calendar app, even when this page is in the background or closed. The event is removed when you pause or reset, and replaced when you resume. Timers under 90 seconds and Lab timers are skipped. Reminders fire on the minute, so expect the alert within about a minute of zero.

Alerts are off.
